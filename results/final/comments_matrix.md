## Summary of Outstanding Items (What Plainly Remains)
1. **Aachen Day-Night Challenge:** Was **not** re-evaluated. Running Aachen requires full 3D structure-from-motion triangulation and `hloc` localization, which was beyond the scope of 2D pair matching. The paper honestly omits Aachen from all empirical claims.
2. **LightGlue Baseline:** Was **not** run on the full benchmark test sets. The paper compares against modern detector-based methods (ALIKED, XFeat) and classical pipelines (SIFT), but does not benchmark matcher-based neural architectures like LightGlue.
3. **Training Seeds:** Models were trained using **one initialization seed per fold** (Seed 0 across Folds 0–4). Multi-seed training variance (e.g. 5 seeds $\times$ 5 folds) was not performed due to training compute limits.
4. **Cross-Dataset Training:** All RL models evaluated on MegaDepth were trained on HPatches (zero-shot transfer); no models were trained directly on MegaDepth.
